## About me

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Brasília — UnB, currently developing research on the ethical evaluation of artificial intelligence systems through automated testing methodologies.

My research investigates how ethical principles such as **Fairness**, **Accountability** and **Transparency** can be operationalized in software engineering, combining systematic literature review, empirical analysis and computational techniques applied to AI-based systems.

I am also an undergraduate student in **Software Engineering** at UnB and hold a degree in **Big Data and Analytical Intelligence** from IESB. My academic and technical background connects software development, data analysis, artificial intelligence, research methodology and critical analysis of emerging technologies.

## Current research

**Master's research:**  
*A Fuzzing-Based Approach for Verifying Ethical Principles in Artificial Intelligence Systems in the Context of Software Engineering.*

This research focuses on investigating computational strategies to support the identification, evaluation and verification of ethical principles in AI systems, especially in software engineering contexts.
